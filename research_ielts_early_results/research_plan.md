# Research question

What is the mechanism behind websites or services claiming to reveal IELTS results before the normal result-release time?

# Subtopics

1. Official IELTS result release and preview process, including the distinction between provisional online results and the official Test Report Form.
2. Plausible technical explanations for unofficial "early result" claims, and security/scam risks when third parties request candidate credentials.

# Synthesis

Separate confirmed official facts from technical inference. Explain that an unofficial service cannot create or grade a result, and avoid claiming a specific hidden endpoint without primary evidence.
