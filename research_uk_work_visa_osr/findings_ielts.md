# IELTS / British Council / IDP findings: IELTS for UKVI One Skill Retake

Research date: 20 August 2026. Official primary sources only.

## Bottom line

Yes: UK Visas and Immigration accepts **IELTS for UKVI One Skill Retake** as part of the Secure English Language Test (SELT) route for UK visa applications, including work routes that require all four skills. IELTS officially announced UKVI approval on 24 October 2023, and current UKVI guidance says that in certain circumstances a candidate who failed one component may be allowed to sit that component again, subject to the provider's terms.

This is **not the same as taking an ordinary IELTS Academic/General Training One Skill Retake**. If the visa route requires a SELT, the underlying full test and the retake must be the UKVI-approved product: **IELTS for UKVI**, taken through an approved provider and location. Ordinary non-SELT IELTS/OSR does not become a UKVI SELT merely because the scores meet the numerical requirement.

## UKVI recognition and work-visa relevance

- IELTS states that UKVI approved IELTS One Skill Retake for SELT requirements for people wishing to live, work or study in the UK. Its announcement specifically says that candidates who sat an **IELTS for UKVI** test may retake Listening, Reading, Writing or Speaking. Source: [IELTS — UK Government approves IELTS One Skill Retake for UK visa applications](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/united-kingdom-government-approves-ielts-one-skill-retake-for-use-with-uk-visa-applications).
- Current UKVI guidance lists Skilled Worker, Health and Care Worker, Scale-up Worker and several other routes among those requiring a four-skill test when the applicant uses the SELT route. It says a failed component may, in certain circumstances, be retaken; the candidate must check the test provider's terms. Source: [GOV.UK — Prove your English language abilities with a SELT](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt).
- The Skilled Worker page says a new applicant using a test must pass an approved SELT and, as of the current page, demonstrate all four skills at CEFR B2. A transitional B1 rule applies to some people who held the visa before 8 January 2026 and are extending/updating it; some applicants can prove English by another route and do not need a new test. Source: [GOV.UK — Skilled Worker visa: Knowledge of English](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/knowledge-of-english).

## Eligibility, timing and test format

- The original test must be a **full IELTS on computer** test at a participating/eligible centre, and the candidate must have received the result before booking the retake.
- For visa use, the original test must specifically be **IELTS for UKVI Academic or IELTS for UKVI General Training**, not ordinary IELTS Academic/General Training.
- Only one skill may be retaken, and only once per original full test.
- The retake must be sat within **60 days of the original test date**.
- The retaken component has the same format, timing, task types, difficulty and scoring as that component in a full IELTS test.
- Availability is centre- and country-dependent. Eligibility should be confirmed in the test-taker portal or with the provider; IELTS's official centre directory supports checking both “One Skill Retake” and “UKVI Approved” status. Sources: [British Council — IELTS One Skill Retake](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/results/one-skill-retake), [IELTS — Test centre directory](https://ielts.org/test-centres), and [IDP — IELTS One Skill Retake guidance](https://ielts.idp.com/prepare/article-how-to-check-band-score-and-improve-results-after-test).

## Result reporting and validity

- British Council says the candidate receives a **new Test Report Form (TRF) within three to five days** after the retake.
- The new TRF shows the new mark for the retaken skill and carries forward the original scores for the other three skills. The original TRF remains available, so the candidate may choose the original or updated result.
- Both the original and updated TRFs remain valid for two years counted from the **original full-test date**, not two years from the retake date.
- For the immigration application, GOV.UK says an IELTS SELT result has a **UKVI number**; UKVI verifies the result online through the provider, and SELT results are valid for two years from award. Sources: [British Council — IELTS One Skill Retake](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/results/one-skill-retake) and [GOV.UK — SELT guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt).

## Ordinary IELTS OSR versus IELTS for UKVI OSR

| Question | Ordinary IELTS Academic/General Training OSR | IELTS for UKVI Academic/General Training OSR |
|---|---|---|
| Is it a UKVI SELT result? | No; it is the non-SELT product. | Yes, when taken under the UKVI-approved arrangements. |
| Can it satisfy a work visa's SELT requirement? | Not by itself. | Yes, subject to the particular visa route's English level and other rules. |
| Underlying full test | Ordinary IELTS on computer at a participating centre. | Full IELTS for UKVI on computer at an eligible UKVI centre. |
| Retake mechanics | One skill, once, within 60 days. | Same mechanics, but the UKVI security/verification chain is preserved. |

The official distinction is important: GOV.UK instructs visa applicants to book the test expressly approved for SELT, naming **“IELTS for UKVI”** (or IELTS Life Skills), and requires an approved test location. Thus, a candidate who originally sat ordinary IELTS should not assume that an ordinary OSR can be submitted as the SELT evidence for a Skilled Worker application. Source: [GOV.UK — SELT guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt).

## Practical caveat

“UK work visa” is not one single route, and English evidence is not always required through IELTS. Before paying for OSR, confirm: (1) the exact visa route, (2) whether the applicant needs a SELT at all or qualifies through a degree/previous successful visa/professional-body assessment, (3) the required CEFR level, and (4) that the portal labels the booking and resulting reference as **IELTS for UKVI / SELT**, not ordinary IELTS OSR.
