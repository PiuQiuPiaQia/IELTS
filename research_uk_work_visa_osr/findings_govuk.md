# GOV.UK / Home Office findings: IELTS One Skill Retake for UK work visas

**Position checked:** 20 August 2026. Only UK Government / Home Office primary sources were used.

## Bottom line

**Yes, a single-component retake can in principle be used for a UK work-visa English-language requirement, including Skilled Worker, but only where it forms part of an approved Secure English Language Test (SELT) result.** For IELTS, the approved four-skill SELT product is **IELTS for UKVI**, not an ordinary/non-UKVI IELTS Academic or General Training test.

GOV.UK does **not** use the product name “IELTS One Skill Retake” on the SELT page. It does, however, expressly state that:

- where multiple components are examined and awarded together, the required scores must be achieved in all relevant components with the **same test-centre provider** as part of the combined assessment; and
- “in certain circumstances”, a candidate who failed one component **may be allowed to sit that component again**, subject to the provider’s terms and conditions.

That is the Home Office’s explicit generic treatment of one-component retakes. The safe operational test is therefore whether the IELTS SELT Consortium issues a valid **UKVI number/digital reference** for the combined IELTS for UKVI result and Home Office can verify it through the SELT system. An ordinary IELTS One Skill Retake result is not enough merely because IELTS is the test brand.

Primary source: UKVI, **Prove your English language abilities with a secure English language test (SELT)** (updated 12 August 2026):
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt

Relevant text appears in the sections “Find an approved test” and “After the test”. The page says the approved IELTS SELT names are **IELTS for UKVI** and **IELTS Life Skills**, and that accepted results must be on the approved list, taken at an approved location, and awarded within the two years before the visa application. IELTS Life Skills only assesses speaking and listening and therefore is not the four-skill product used for Skilled Worker.

## Skilled Worker level and IELTS scores

Under the current Immigration Rules, a Skilled Worker must show the required CEFR level in **all four components: reading, writing, speaking and listening**.

- **New / initial Skilled Worker application under the rules from 8 January 2026:** CEFR **B2** in all four components.
- **Extension where the applicant’s most recent permission was Skilled Worker and it was granted subject to B1:** CEFR **B1** remains sufficient.
- The wording is tied to the applicant’s most recent Skilled Worker grant. A person moving from another route (for example Graduate) into Skilled Worker after 8 January 2026 does not get the B1 extension protection merely because they already hold UK leave.

| CEFR level | IELTS for UKVI minimum |
|---|---|
| B1 | **4.0 in each** of listening, speaking, reading and writing |
| B2 | **5.5 in each** of listening, speaking, reading and writing |

## Why the combined retake result can meet the rule

Appendix English Language, EL 6.1, requires a **valid digital reference number from an approved provider** showing that the applicant passed an approved English-language test at the required level in **each required component**, within the two years before application. It does not require all four components to be sat again on one day. Read together with the SELT guidance expressly allowing a failed component to be sat again in certain circumstances, this supports acceptance of an approved-provider combined single-retake result.

Primary source: Immigration Rules, **Appendix English Language**, EL 6.1:  
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-english-language

This is a rule-based inference from the Home Office wording because GOV.UK does not separately name “IELTS One Skill Retake”. It should not be broadened to a non-SELT IELTS result or to mixing results from different providers.

## Practical decision rule for the applicant

Before relying on the result, check all of the following:

1. The original full test was **IELTS for UKVI**, not ordinary IELTS.
2. It was taken at an approved SELT location.
3. The one-component retake is offered under the IELTS SELT Consortium’s terms for that test and is combined into a verifiable UKVI SELT result.
4. The new result has a **UKVI number/digital reference** and shows the required score in every component.
5. The result is within two years of the visa application.
6. The correct Skilled Worker threshold applies: normally B2 / IELTS 5.5 each for a new application from 8 January 2026, or B1 / IELTS 4.0 each only for the protected same-route extension described above.

## Other ways a Skilled Worker may meet English

IELTS is not always necessary. Appendix English Language also covers, among other routes:

- having already proved English at the level required for the current application in a previous successful immigration application (EL 3.1);
- nationality of a listed majority-English-speaking country (EL 4.1);
- an eligible UK degree or qualifying overseas English-taught degree, with the required evidence (EL 5.1–5.4);
- certain UK school English qualifications (EL 7.1–7.2); and
- for a doctor, dentist, nurse, midwife or veterinarian, an English assessment accepted by the relevant regulated professional body (EL 9.1).
