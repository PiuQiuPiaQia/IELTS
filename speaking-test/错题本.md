# 口语错题本

只记**说错的句子**，按类型分组。左边是当时说的，右边是正确说法。
来源：10 份录音批改（8 份 Part 2 + 2 份完整模考）。

---

## 1. 时态一致性 ⬅️ 最常错

**一条规则：开口前先定「这段讲过去还是现在」，中间不换。**

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `I feel relieved` | `I **felt** relieved` |
| `as for how I feel about the change` | `as for how I **felt**` |
| `we can fix it more quickly` | `we **could** fix` |
| `I thought I made a mistake` | `I thought I **had made** a mistake` |
| `our feature released on time` | `our feature **was released** on time` |
| `I thought it's a difficult task` | `I thought it **was**` |
| `he can speak some simple words` | `he **could** speak` |
| `Finally, he becomes successful` | `he **became** successful` |
| `I first know him` | `I first **knew** him` |
| `What impressed me the most is` | `...**was**` |
| `I can ask help from others` | `I **could** ask for help` |
| `I always spent my free time to relax` | `I always **spend** my free time relaxing` |
| `it had many offices`（客观事实） | `it **has** many offices` |
| `that came to my mind` | `that **comes** to my mind`（固定开场用现在时） |

---

## 2. 句式塌 / 缺动词 ⬅️ 讲得快时最常丢

**一条规则：先在心里定好「主语 + 动词」，再开口。**

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `changing jobs also a challenge` | `changing jobs **is** also a challenge` |
| `changing jobs are good` | `changing jobs **is** good` |
| `is some people like making a plan` | `**Some** people like making a plan` |
| `If a small task we did not need` | `If **it is** a small task, we do not need` |
| `it may be waste our time` | `it may **waste** our time` |
| `it may be not a good choice` | `it **may not be** a good choice` |
| `I have to a lot of changes` | `I **have made** a lot of changes` |
| `do not need others helpful` | `do not need **others' help**` |
| `make everyone like a want around him` | `**makes** everyone **want to be** around him` |

---

## 3. 介词 / 搭配

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `asked help` | `asked **for** help` |
| `asked my budget` | `asked **about** my budget` |
| `shares ... to others` | `shares ... **with** others` |
| `complains with` | `complains **about**` |
| `recommended two watches for me` | `recommended ... **to** me` |
| `connect the watch with my phone` | `connect it **to** my phone` |
| `by cardboard`（连错 2 次） | `**from** cardboard` |
| `a new feature about our system` | `a new feature **for** our system` |
| `release it for some small groups` | `release it **to** some small groups` |
| `photos about it` | `photos **of** it` |

---

## 4. 冠词

**规则：单数可数名词前面必须有 a / an / the。**

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `in shop` | `in **a** shop` |
| `to Xiaomi shop` | `to **a / the** Xiaomi shop` |
| `of online shopping system` | `of **an** online shopping system` |
| `I live in big city` | `I live in **a** big city` |
| `has unexpected problem` | `has **an** unexpected problem` |
| `from TV show` | `from **a** TV show` |
| `I wonderful experience` | `**a** wonderful experience` |

---

## 5. 三单 / 主谓一致

**规则：he / she / everyone 作主语，一般现在时动词加 -s。**

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `he always give a hand` | `he always **gives** a hand` |
| `he always help others` | `he always **helps** others` |
| `he always share things` | `he always **shares** things` |
| `he always answer with humor` | `he always **answers** with humor` |
| `everyone trust him and like him` | `everyone **trusts** him and **likes** him` |

---

## 6. tell 语序

**`tell` 是双宾语动词，不加 to：`tell me a story`。**

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `tells jokes to me` | `tells **me** jokes` |
| `told some stories to me` | `told **me** some stories` |

---

## 7. 单复数 / 比较级 / 用词

| 说错的 | 正确 |
|---|---|
| `many happy persons` | `many happy **people**` |
| `when problem happens` | `when **problems happen**` |
| `a lot of good experience` | `a lot of good **experiences**` |
| `more slow` | `**slower**` |
| `get a high salary` | `get a **higher** salary` |
| `improve our life quality` | `improve our **quality of life**` |
| `learn a lot of new knowledge` | `**gain** a lot of new knowledge` |
| `find how programs work` | `**find out** how programs work` |
| `make a success` | `**succeed**` |
| `it was simple and also taught me` | `**he** also taught me` |
| `a simple way that we can relax` | `a simple way **for us** to relax` |
| `As far as I admire him` | `**As for why** I admire him` |
| `I had a travel to Shanghai` | `I **traveled** to Shanghai` |
| `complete the step by step` | `**completed it** step by step` |

---

## 8. 讲得好的地方（抄下来当模板）

- 转场：`As for how I feel about [人/物], I really admire him, and there are three reasons.`
- 第三点：`What's more, ...`
- 条件式开头：`It depends on their personality.` / `For introverts, they prefer to work by themselves.`
- 正反结构：`... is good for us, because ... But on the other side, ... So people should consider their own situation.`
