## Official facts versus third-party claims

- **Treat as authoritative:** the Cambridge catalogue's product title, publication month/year, ISBN, format and statement that the material is official/authentic.
- **Do not treat as publication authority:** retailer listings, preparation websites, forums, social-media posts, downloadable scans or search-result snippets. They may help locate a title, but their dates and naming can reflect preorder status, regional stock or unofficial wording.
- The conclusions above do **not** depend on third-party publication claims. A Google result was used only to locate the current Cambridge URL after an older guessed catalogue path returned 404; every reported metadata field was then verified on Cambridge's own live catalogue.
- No official Cambridge catalogue entry later than IELTS 21 was found or shown ahead of IELTS 21 in the publication-date-sorted catalogue as of 6 August 2026. This is a time-bounded observation, not a claim that a future IELTS 22 has never been announced elsewhere.

## Implication for template testing

For a check against the newest Cambridge material, prioritize **IELTS 21 Academic Task 2** prompts. If General Training writing is also in scope, note that GT Task 2 uses the same essay task family but the prompt set is published in the separate General Training component above.
