# Research Plan: Recent Cambridge IELTS Task 2 Prompts

## Main question

Do 6-10 recent, verifiably Cambridge IELTS Writing Task 2 prompts fit the project's merged four-paragraph template, and what prompt-type differences must remain explicit?

## Subtopics

1. **Newest editions and primary attribution**
   - Establish the newest verifiable Cambridge IELTS books available by August 2026.
   - Find official publisher/catalogue evidence for edition, Academic/General Training status, and test count.

2. **Recent prompt collection**
   - Collect exact or carefully transcribed Task 2 prompts from the newest books.
   - Cover opinion, discuss-both-views, advantages/disadvantages or outweigh, and two-question formats.
   - Record book/test attribution and whether Academic or General Training is known.

3. **Reliability audit**
   - Prefer publisher previews, searchable book previews, or multiple independent transcriptions.
   - Label single secondary-source transcriptions as medium or low confidence.
   - Preserve source URLs and distinguish book-level evidence from prompt-level evidence.

## Synthesis

Create `findings_recent_prompts.md` with 6-10 prompts, prompt type, attribution, source reliability, URLs, and concise notes on how each tests the merged template.
