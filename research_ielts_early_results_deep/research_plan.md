# Main question

What concrete mechanism, if any, supports paid or third-party IELTS "early result checking" services used by candidates in mainland China?

# Subtopics

1. Marketed services and user-visible workflow: identify actual claims, required fields, promised lead time, and whether the service appears to be prediction, portal automation, or alleged database access.
2. Technical evidence: inspect official candidate-result portals, restricted institution result services, public documentation, and any relevant public code or archived pages for evidence of separate back-end publication and front-end release timing.
3. Official security and privacy position: establish which identifiers are sensitive, whether result data is provisional before release, and what official channels say about third-party access.

# Synthesis

Rank explanations by evidence. Distinguish direct evidence, service-provider claims, user anecdotes, and inference. Do not identify a hidden endpoint or describe bypass steps unless supported by public primary documentation, and do not expose personal-data handling methods.
