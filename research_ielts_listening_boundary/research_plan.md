# Research question

How often and by how much can the IELTS Listening raw-score-to-band boundary vary between test versions, especially around Band 5.5?

# Subtopics

1. Official IELTS scoring guidance: whether raw-score boundaries are fixed or may vary slightly between versions.
2. Practical implication for a candidate scoring around Band 5.5: how much confidence to place in a possible boundary shift.

# Synthesis

Use official IELTS or test-partner sources to distinguish small test-form variation from a large or discretionary score adjustment, and avoid inventing a numerical probability that IELTS does not publish.
